# Oryzo — Style Reference
> quiet editorial on rice paper

**Theme:** light
**Best for:** minimal aesthetic, editorial, craft, wellness

Oryzo is typeset on rice paper: a soft warm-white canvas, ink-black serif headlines, and generous margins that make every word feel considered. Color barely appears — a single muted moss green (#5a7a5a) for links and quiet accents. The rhythm is literary: narrow measure, tall line-height, and sections separated by space rather than rules. It feels like a well-printed essay.

## Color Palette

| Role | Value | Usage |
|------|-------|-------|
| Rice Paper | `#faf9f6` | Page canvas |
| Ink Black | `#1a1a18` | Headlines, primary text |
| Soft Gray | `#6f6f68` | Body copy |
| Moss Green | `#5a7a5a` | Links, quiet accents |
| Card White | `#ffffff` | Card surfaces |
| Hairline | `#e8e6df` | Borders, dividers |

## Typography

- **Headlines**: Georgia (substitute: serif), weight 700, 40-56px, tracking -0.01em
- **Body**: Inter (substitute: system-ui), weight 400, 16-17px, line-height 1.75
- Kicker: 12px, weight 600, uppercase, 0.1em tracking, moss green

## Spacing & Shapes

- Card radius: 8px
- Button radius: 9999px (soft pill)
- Section gap: 96-128px
- Page max-width: 720px for text, 1080px for layouts
- 1px #e8e6df hairlines; no shadows

## Key Patterns

- Hero: kicker → serif headline → deck → moss-text link CTA
- Long-form measure: narrow 640-720px column for prose
- Cards: white on rice paper, 8px radius, hairline border
- Sections separated by whitespace, not rules
- Serif headlines throughout — the editorial voice

## Do's
- Serif display type, narrow text measure, tall line-height
- Rice-paper warm white canvas, never pure white
- Moss green used sparingly for links only
- Whitespace as the primary separator

## Don'ts
- No bold colors or heavy graphics
- No shadows or gradients
- No wide walls of text — keep measure narrow
- No sans-serif headlines
