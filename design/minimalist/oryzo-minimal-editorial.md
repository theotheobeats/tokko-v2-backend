# ORYZO AI — Style Reference
> Darkroom product editorial

**Theme:** dark
**Best for:** tech, beauty, fashion

## Color Palette
Deep dark studio background. Single product illuminated — chiaroscuro lighting approach.

## Typography
Editorial serif + clean sans. Generous spacing.

## Spacing & Shapes
Minimal borders, product-focused layouts.

## Key Patterns
Darkroom photography aesthetic — one product dramatically lit against near-black. Editorial typography. Product IS the hero.

## Do's
Dark backgrounds. One focal product. Editorial type treatment.

## Don'ts
No busy layouts. No multiple competing visuals.
