# Hyperstudio — Style Reference
> blueprint scratched into obsidian

**Theme:** dark
**Best for:** tech, gadget

## Color Palette
Obsidian black, blueprint blue lines. Technical diagram aesthetic.

## Typography
Monospaced + technical sans. Blueprint/terminal feel.

## Spacing & Shapes
Sharp, technical, diagram-like borders.

## Key Patterns
Blueprints on obsidian. Technical precision. Architectural diagrams as visual language.

## Do's
Blueprint aesthetic. Technical monospaced labels. Dark canvas.

## Don'ts
No softness. No rounded cards. No warm colors.
