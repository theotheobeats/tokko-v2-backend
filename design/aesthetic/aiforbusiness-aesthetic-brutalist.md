# AI for Business — Style Reference
> brutalist editorial showroom on raw concrete

**Theme:** light
**Best for:** bold aesthetic, tech, service, brands that want to look unapologetic

AI for Business is poured concrete and black Helvetica: raw gray surfaces, hard 90° corners, and typography so bold it feels structural. One violent accent — signal yellow (#facc15) — slashes across the page for CTAs and key words. Nothing is soft, nothing is rounded, nothing whispers. The layout is a grid of heavy blocks with thick black rules, like an architectural blueprint crossed with a protest poster.

## Color Palette

| Role | Value | Usage |
|------|-------|-------|
| Raw Concrete | `#e5e5e0` | Page canvas, alternating sections |
| Slab Gray | `#f5f5f2` | Card/block surfaces |
| Carbon Black | `#171717` | Headlines, body, thick rules |
| Signal Yellow | `#facc15` | CTAs, highlighted words, accent blocks ONLY |
| Mid Concrete | `#a3a39e` | Secondary text, captions |
| Hard Rule | `#171717` | 2-3px black dividers |

## Typography

- **Primary**: Inter / Helvetica Neue, weights 400-900
- Display: 56-80px, weight 800-900, tracking -0.02em, UPPERCASE for hero — shout it
- Section heading: 36-48px, weight 800
- Body: 16px, weight 400-500, line-height 1.55
- Labels: 12-13px, weight 700, uppercase, 0.08em tracking

## Spacing & Shapes

- Corner radius: 0px (hard edges, non-negotiable)
- Section gap: 0 — sections butt against each other, separated by 2-3px black rules
- Page max-width: 1280px
- Generous internal padding (48-64px) inside each block
- Thick 2-3px solid black borders on blocks and buttons

## Key Patterns

- Hero: massive uppercase black headline on concrete, one yellow CTA block
- Highlight key words in headlines with a #facc15 background swash
- Buttons: solid black or solid yellow rectangles, 0 radius, bold uppercase text
- Sections separated by thick black horizontal rules, not whitespace
- Cards: flat gray blocks with 2px black borders, no shadow, no rounding

## Do's
- 0px border-radius everywhere — sharp is the aesthetic
- Signal yellow only for actions and headline swashes
- Weight 800-900 uppercase headlines
- Thick black rules and borders as structure

## Don'ts
- No rounded corners of any kind
- No shadows, gradients, or blur
- No pastels or second accent colors
- No centered delicate layouts — heavy, left-aligned, blocky
