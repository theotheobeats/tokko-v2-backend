# AI for Business — Style Reference
> Brutalist editorial showroom on raw concrete

**Theme:** light
**Best for:** tech, service, bold brands

## Color Palette
Raw concrete grays. Bold black type. Single vibrant accent (yellow/orange).

## Typography
Brutalist bold sans. Extreme weights. Raw typography.

## Spacing & Shapes
Raw blocks. No rounding. Brutalist forms.

## Key Patterns
Business AI as brutalist architecture. Raw concrete. Bold statements. Single accent color punches.

## Do's
Raw concrete grays. Bold type. Single accent punch.

## Don'ts
No softness. No rounded corners. No pastels. No multiple accents.
