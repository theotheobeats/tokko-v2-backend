# Authkit — Style Reference
> frosted glass cathedral at midnight

**Theme:** dark
**Best for:** bold aesthetic, tech, beauty, premium services

Authkit builds pages like a cathedral of frosted glass at midnight: a deep violet-black canvas, panels of translucent glass floating above it, and neon edge-light tracing every surface. Glow is the material — a vivid violet (#8b5cf6) and electric blue (#3b82f6) bleed softly around CTAs and cards. Type is big, bright, and airy against the dark. Everything feels premium, nocturnal, and slightly futuristic.

## Color Palette

| Role | Value | Usage |
|------|-------|-------|
| Midnight Canvas | `#0b0614` | Page background — near-black violet |
| Glass Surface | `rgba(255,255,255,0.06)` | Frosted cards (use with soft border) |
| Neon Violet | `#8b5cf6` | Primary CTA, glows, key accents |
| Electric Blue | `#3b82f6` | Secondary accents, links |
| Starlight Text | `#f5f3ff` | Headlines, primary text |
| Dim Lavender | `#a5a3c2` | Body copy, secondary text |
| Edge Light | `rgba(139,92,246,0.4)` | Glowing card borders |

## Typography

- **Primary**: Inter (substitute: system-ui), weights 400-700
- Display: 56-72px, weight 700, tracking -0.03em — luminous and large
- Section heading: 34-40px, weight 700
- Body: 16px, weight 400, line-height 1.6, in dim lavender
- Eyebrow: 13px, weight 600, uppercase, 0.1em tracking, in neon violet

## Spacing & Shapes

- Card radius: 20px (soft cathedral arches)
- Button radius: 9999px (pill)
- Section gap: 100-140px
- Page max-width: 1120px
- Borders: 1px edge-light glow, no hard lines

## Key Patterns

- Hero: glowing eyebrow → huge starlight headline → violet pill CTA with soft glow (box-shadow: 0 0 32px rgba(139,92,246,0.5))
- Cards: frosted glass (semi-transparent white) + 1px violet edge glow, 20px radius
- CTAs: pill, filled #8b5cf6, surrounded by neon bloom
- Section accents: radial violet glow behind key sections (box-shadow or large soft radial)
- Alternate deep canvas with slightly lifted violet-tinted bands

## Do's
- Glow generously: box-shadow blooms around CTAs and cards
- Frosted glass (rgba white at 4-8%) for all elevated surfaces
- Big luminous headlines in near-white
- Violet + blue as the only two accent hues

## Don'ts
- No flat matte surfaces — everything glows or is glass
- No warm colors (no orange, yellow, red)
- No small cramped type — keep it large and airy
- No harsh solid borders — edges are light, not lines
