# Authkit — Style Reference
> Frosted glass cathedral at midnight

**Theme:** dark
**Best for:** tech, beauty, premium services

## Color Palette
Deep midnight purple/blue. Frosted glass white. Neon violet/blue glows.

## Typography
Clean sans on frosted glass. Elevated, premium.

## Spacing & Shapes
Frosted glass cards (backdrop-blur). Glowing borders. Cathedral proportions.

## Key Patterns
Auth UI as frosted glass cathedral. Depth through glass layers. Neon edge glows. Verticality.

## Do's
Frosted glass layers. Neon edge glows. Midnight purple backgrounds.

## Don'ts
No flat surfaces. No warm colors. No small type.
