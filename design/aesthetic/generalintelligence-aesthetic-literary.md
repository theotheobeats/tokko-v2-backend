# General Intelligence Company — Style Reference
> Literary journal beside a bonfire

**Theme:** dark
**Best for:** editorial, craft, service

## Color Palette
Deep warm blacks. Bonfire amber/orange glow. Parchment accents.

## Typography
Literary serif + warm sans. Journal feel. Generous line heights.

## Spacing & Shapes
Book-like layouts. Warm organic shapes. Literary proportions.

## Key Patterns
AI company as literary press. Bonfire warmth on dark. Serif authority. Journal layouts.

## Do's
Literary serif. Bonfire amber glow. Dark warm canvas.

## Don'ts
No cold blues. No tech clichés. No neon green.
