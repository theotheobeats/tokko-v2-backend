# Hungry Tiger — Style Reference
> Turmeric-bright graffiti on a warm kitchen wall

**Theme:** light
**Best for:** food, creative, bold brands

## Color Palette
Warm kitchen cream. Turmeric yellow. Bold red/orange accents. Graffiti energy.

## Typography
Bold display + warm sans. Energetic, street-food energy.

## Spacing & Shapes
Organic, warm, slightly chaotic. Street art aesthetic.

## Key Patterns
Restaurant as street art. Turmeric yellow dominates. Bold graffiti typography. Menu as visual texture.

## Do's
Turmeric yellow #e8b308. Warm kitchen backgrounds. Graffiti energy.

## Don'ts
No cold colors. No formal layouts. No minimalism.
