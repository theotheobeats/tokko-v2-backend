# Hungry Tiger — Style Reference
> bold street-food energy, chili red on charcoal

**Theme:** dark
**Best for:** bold aesthetic, food & beverage, restaurants, F&B brands

Hungry Tiger is a night-market food stall with neon signage: deep charcoal canvas, blazing chili red (#ef3e2e), and warm golden yellow (#f5b301) like heat off a grill. Type is heavy and appetizing — big rounded headlines that shout the menu. Cards are chunky with 16px corners, CTAs are bright and urgent, and the whole page feels hot, fast, and delicious.

## Color Palette

| Role | Value | Usage |
|------|-------|-------|
| Night Charcoal | `#17130f` | Page canvas |
| Stall Panel | `#221b14` | Card surfaces |
| Chili Red | `#ef3e2e` | Primary CTA, hot accents |
| Golden Yellow | `#f5b301` | Price highlights, secondary accent |
| Cream Text | `#faf3e8` | Headlines, primary text |
| Warm Gray | `#a89a89` | Body copy |
| Hairline | `#352a1f` | Borders |

## Typography

- **Primary**: Inter / Nunito (substitute: system-ui), weights 400-800
- Display: 52-72px, weight 800, tracking -0.02em — big and appetizing
- Section heading: 34-42px, weight 800
- Body: 16px, weight 400, line-height 1.6
- Prices/labels: 14px, weight 700, golden yellow

## Spacing & Shapes

- Card radius: 16px (chunky, friendly)
- Button radius: 9999px (pill)
- Section gap: 88-120px
- Page max-width: 1100px
- 1px #352a1f hairlines; soft glow on red CTAs

## Key Patterns

- Hero: huge heavy headline → chili-red pill CTA with warm glow (box-shadow: 0 0 24px rgba(239,62,46,0.4))
- Menu/product cards: #221b14 chunky cards, 16px radius, golden price tags
- CTAs: solid chili red, bold white text, pill shape
- Golden yellow for prices, ratings, and "hot" badges
- Alternate charcoal and stall-panel tones for rhythm

## Do's
- Chili red for actions, golden yellow for prices/highlights
- Heavy weight-800 headlines that shout the food
- Chunky 16px cards, pill buttons
- Warm dark canvas that feels like evening street food

## Don'ts
- No cool blues or clinical whites
- No thin delicate type — go heavy
- No sharp minimal corners
- No muted pastel palette — this is bold and hot
