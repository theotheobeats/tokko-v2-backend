# Awesomic — Style Reference
> editorial bold on warm cream

**Theme:** light
**Best for:** warm aesthetic, agencies, creative studios, portfolios

Awesomic is a design-studio portfolio printed on warm cream: big confident serif-ish headlines, a punchy tomato red (#ef4444) for accents, and playful editorial layouts that break the grid just enough. It balances warmth and boldness — cream canvas, near-black ink, generous whitespace, and color used like a rubber stamp. Corners are soft (16px), and every section feels like a magazine spread.

## Color Palette

| Role | Value | Usage |
|------|-------|-------|
| Warm Cream | `#faf6f0` | Page canvas |
| Studio Ink | `#181510` | Headlines, primary text |
| Tomato Red | `#ef4444` | CTAs, accent stamps, highlights |
| Warm Gray | `#7a7268` | Body copy |
| Card White | `#fffefa` | Card surfaces |
| Hairline | `#e9e2d6` | Borders |
| Blush Tint | `#fdeaea` | Highlight panels |

## Typography

- **Headlines**: Georgia / Charter (substitute: serif), weight 700, 48-72px, tracking -0.02em
- **Body**: Inter (substitute: system-ui), weight 400, 16px, line-height 1.6
- Kicker: 13px, weight 700, uppercase, 0.08em tracking, tomato red

## Spacing & Shapes

- Card radius: 16px
- Button radius: 9999px (friendly pill)
- Section gap: 96-128px
- Page max-width: 1160px
- 1px #e9e2d6 hairlines; no shadows

## Key Patterns

- Hero: kicker → huge serif headline with one red-stamped word → pill CTA
- Cards: white on cream, 16px radius, hairline border
- CTAs: tomato red pill, bold white text
- Editorial rhythm: alternate cream and blush panels
- Serif headlines with selective red emphasis on key words

## Do's
- Warm cream canvas, never pure white
- Tomato red as a single punchy accent
- Big serif headlines with personality
- Soft 16px corners, friendly pill buttons

## Don'ts
- No cold grays or blues
- No shadows or gradients
- No timid small headlines
- No cluttered layouts — keep editorial whitespace
