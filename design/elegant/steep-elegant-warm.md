# Steep — Style Reference
> artisan tea house, warm minimal

**Theme:** light
**Best for:** warm aesthetic, food & beverage, craft, wellness, lifestyle

Steep is a quiet tea house: warm oat-milk canvas, deep roasted-brown ink, and a muted matcha green (#6b8f5e) as the only accent. Everything is soft, rounded, and calm — 20px card corners, pill buttons, generous padding, and serif headlines that feel hand-set. It evokes craft and care, like a label on a hand-packed tin of tea.

## Color Palette

| Role | Value | Usage |
|------|-------|-------|
| Oat Canvas | `#f7f3ec` | Page background |
| Roasted Ink | `#33302a` | Headlines, primary text |
| Matcha Green | `#6b8f5e` | CTAs, accents, highlights |
| Warm Taupe | `#8a8175` | Body copy |
| Card Cream | `#fffdf8` | Card surfaces |
| Hairline | `#e6dfd2` | Borders |
| Matcha Tint | `#eef2ea` | Soft highlight panels |

## Typography

- **Headlines**: Georgia (substitute: serif), weight 700, 40-56px
- **Body**: Inter (substitute: system-ui), weight 400, 16px, line-height 1.7
- Kicker: 12px, weight 600, uppercase, 0.08em tracking, matcha green

## Spacing & Shapes

- Card radius: 20px (very soft)
- Button radius: 9999px (pill)
- Section gap: 88-120px
- Page max-width: 1080px
- 1px #e6dfd2 hairlines; no shadows

## Key Patterns

- Hero: kicker → serif headline → deck → matcha pill CTA
- Cards: cream on oat canvas, 20px radius, hairline border
- CTAs: matcha green pill, white text
- Alternate oat and matcha-tint panels for gentle rhythm
- Generous padding inside cards (32-40px) for a calm feel

## Do's
- Warm oat canvas and roasted-brown ink
- Matcha green as the single calm accent
- Very soft 20px corners and pill buttons
- Serif headlines for craft credibility

## Don'ts
- No bright or saturated colors
- No sharp corners or hard edges
- No shadows or gradients
- No cold blue/gray tones
