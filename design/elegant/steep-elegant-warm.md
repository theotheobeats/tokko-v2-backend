# Steep — Style Reference
> serif analytics on warm paper

**Theme:** light
**Best for:** finance, service, editorial

## Color Palette
Warm paper canvas, dark ink text. Beige/cream backgrounds. Rich navy or deep brown accents.

## Typography
Serif display + clean sans body. Editorial, literary feel. Generous line heights.

## Spacing & Shapes
Moderate rounding, card-based layouts with subtle borders.

## Key Patterns
Data dashboards that feel like printed financial reports. Serif headlines give weight. Warm paper backgrounds make data approachable.

## Do's
Serif for headlines. Warm paper backgrounds. Data + editorial hybrid.

## Don'ts
No neon. No pure white. No cold grays.
