# Mercury — Style Reference
> Alpine banking at blue hour

**Theme:** light
**Best for:** finance, service, tech

## Color Palette
Clean white with alpine blue accents. Cool, crisp, trustworthy. Blue hour photography.

## Typography
Clean sans-serif. Precise, trustworthy. Professional but warm.

## Spacing & Shapes
Clean cards, moderate radii, professional spacing.

## Key Patterns
Banking dashboard as alpine landscape. Clean, fresh, airy. Blue hour gradients in photography.

## Do's
Alpine blue as trust color. Clean white. Professional spacing.

## Don'ts
No clutter. No warm earth tones. No playful elements.
